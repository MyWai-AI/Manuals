The **Anomalies** page displays the complete collection of anomalies generated by algorithm inference executed on Edge devices.

In the platform, an **Anomaly** represents a specific _time range within a continuous data stream_ during which an inference was executed and the algorithm detected a condition that must be reported.

<Info>
Inference feedbacks on **Events** are not stored as Anomalies in the platform.
</Info>

Reported information can be of two types:

* **Anomaly**: indicates that an abnormal condition was detected within the specified time range.
* **Label**: a classification label assigned to the time range by a classification algorithm.

Each anomaly is defined by:

* The event timestamp.
* The associated Equipment on which the anomaly was detected.
* The list of involved Measures.
* An optional associated label.

Anomalies can also be visualized by creating **Anomalies widgets** within [Reports (Dashboards)](/reporting/reports).

### View Anomaly Details

Clicking on the magnifying glass icon on the anomaly row in the table, a popup modal is shown that provides the visualization of the timeseries of the data involved in the anomaly.

### Certification on IOTA Blockchain

Anomalies can be certified on the IOTA Blockchain to provide **cryptographic proof of integrity, timestamping, and immutability**.

Certifying anomalies delivers the following concrete values:

1. **Tamper-proof evidence**
   * The anomaly metadata (timestamp, equipment ID, measures, label, algorithm reference) is hashed and anchored on the ledger.
   * Any post-hoc modification is detectable.
2. **Trusted timestamping**
   * The blockchain timestamp proves _when_ the anomaly was generated, independently of the platform or Edge device clocks.
3. **Auditability and compliance**
   * Enables verifiable audit trails for regulatory, industrial, or contractual compliance (e.g. quality assurance, safety, AI accountability).
4. **Non-repudiation**
   * The producer of the anomaly (Edge + algorithm version) cannot deny having generated it.
5. **Algorithm accountability**
   * Certification can include references to algorithm version, model hash, and configuration, enabling traceability of AI behavior over time.

To be enabled to certify an anomaly on the blockchain, ensure to have configured the [Blockchain](/configuration/blockchain-v3.0) properly in the platform.
