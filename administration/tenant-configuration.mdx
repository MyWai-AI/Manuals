# Tenant configuration

Upon starting your journey with MYWAI, you have to correctly set up the details related to your company. This process not only customizes your user interface but also ensures that communication and specific functionalities align seamlessly with your organization.

## **Tenant Configuration**

When you log in for the first time, you'll be presented with a company configuration form. Please fill in the following details with care:

* **Logo:** Upload your company's logo. This image will be displayed on the platform's interface and on potential external communications.
* **Name:** Enter the official name of your company.
* **VAT Number:** Provide your VAT identification number, ensuring unique identification for fiscal and legal purposes.
* **Responsible Party Email:** Specify the email address of the main point of contact or the primary responsible individual within your organization.

### Sites and Areas

In addition to this general information about your company, an essential part of the setup involves breaking down your company into distinct Sites and Areas.

* **List of Sites:** Your company's operations might span across multiple locations or sites. Here, you can insert these locations. At least one site must be added, but you can as many as needed. For each site, you'll have the option to specify:
  * **Site Name:** E.g., "Headquarters", "Branch A", "Manufacturing Plant", etc.
  * **Site Address:** The physical address of the particular site.
  * **Areas for the Site:** (Optional) Every site can have various departments or areas that serve specific functions. For each area linked with the site please provide:
    * **Area Name:** For example, "Sales", "Technical Support", "Research & Development", and so on.
    * **Description:** A brief descriptive summary about the area or its role within that specific site.

Entities like Equipment, Sensors, and Edge devices can later be tied to physical sites, including, if specified, their respective areas. Such configuration facilitates the coordination for a more structured and efficient entity management system.

## **Entities Assignment to Sites and Areas**

The various sites and areas you've defined will be used to allocate physical entities, such as _equipment_, _sensors_, and _edge_ devices. Additionally, _roles_ can be assigned to grant specific permissions exclusively to certain sites or areas. This structure enables organized and efficient management of these entities based on their respective locations.

## **Editing Company Information**

It's important to understand that the information provided during the initial setup is not set in stone.
