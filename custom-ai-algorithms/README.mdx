# Custom AI Algorithms

The MYWAI platform provides an environment for integrating and running **custom AI algorithms**.

Recognizing the diverse needs of today's AI applications, the platform has been designed to support both **cloud-based computations** and **real-time processing on Edge devices**.

This manual aims to offer a guide on how to integrate externally crafted AI algorithms into the MYWAI ecosystem. Users will gain insights into the flexibility of deploying their algorithms either on the central MYWAI cloud platform or directly on an Edge computing device.

By following the outlined steps and best practices, users can effectively harness the platform's capabilities for their specific AI needs. Let's delve into the process of integrating and optimizing your AI algorithms within MYWAI.
