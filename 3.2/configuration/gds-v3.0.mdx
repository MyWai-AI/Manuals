---
title: "GDS"
---

The MYWAI **GDS** (Global Discovery Server) issues the OPC UA certificates used by an Edge and by the equipment connected to it. It is not installed on its own: it starts on the Edge machine together with the Edge. See [Edge Installation](/3.2/edge-operations/edge-installation-v3.0/index#the-gds).

This page lists the GDS instances that have contacted the platform.

When you start an Edge, its GDS appears in this list as a join request. Users with sufficient permissions can **accept** or **reject** it with the corresponding buttons. After you accept it, the platform sends the signed certificate to that GDS.
