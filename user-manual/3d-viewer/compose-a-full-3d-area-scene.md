# Compose a full 3D Area scene

One of the major functionalities available in the Platform with the viewer is the capability to compose **3D Scene by mixing&#x20;together the 3D Models from the Company's areas and their assigned Equipment** (Equipments can be&#x20;assigned to a Site and a specific Area).

## **1. Configure the 3D model of an Area**

Upload a 3D model to an Area, form this page:\
![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (44).png>)\
After uploading and saving the Company Info, now in the homepage the Area should be visible and display a&#x20;3D button underneath it. By clicking it the 3D Viewer should be visible and display the uploaded file.\
![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (45).png>)

## **2. Configure the 3D models of the Equipments**

Create one or more equipments and assign them to a 3D model. Assign the Equipment to Site & Area of the step above.\
![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (46).png>)\
\
After clicking on the 3D button, you have 2 options:

1. Generate a 3D model automatically from a plain image. This task is time consuming so will run as a background job. The user can verify the success of this operation in the Background Job page
2. Upload a ._glb_, _gltf_ file.\
   ![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (47).png>)

## **3. Positioning Equipment 3D Models in the Scene**

Once an Area has a 3D model and an Equipment has its own 3D model assigned to that Area, the user can return to the Homepage and open the Viewer associated with that Area.

When the viewer loads, **additional buttons** become available in **toolbar #2**, enabling the placement and adjustment of each Equipment’s 3D model within the Area scene.

After positioning all models, **save the configuration** using the **Save Scene** button located at the top of the same toolbar.
