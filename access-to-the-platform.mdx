## Access via SSO (Auth0 or Keycloak)

All users access MYWAI via [Auth0](https://auth0.com/) o Keycloak SSO systems. Both are popular solutions for identity and access management. MYWAI offers three options:

1. Using the default Keycloak system provided by MYWAI.
2. Integrating with your organization's existing Auth0 setup, if you already utilize Auth0 for single-sign-on and authentication in your company.
3. Integrating with your organization's existing Keycloak setup, if you already utilize Keycloak for single-sign-on and authentication in your company.

Here's a breakdown of the two scenarios:

_1. Using_ MYWAI_'s_ Keycloak _system_

* If your organization opts for the Keycloak system integrated with MYWAI, users can access the platform by entering their Keycloak account credentials. After typing the username and password in the designated fields, simply click on the "Log In" button to access the platform.
* Upon activation of your MYWAI platform, you should have received the Administrator account credentials. Use these to log in and manage the platform.

_2. Using a customer-specific Auth0 or_ Keycloak _account_

* If your organization has its own Auth0 or Keycloak setup, it can be integrated with MYWAI. In this scenario, when accessing MYWAI, users will be redirected to their organization's Auth0 or Keycloak  login page. If already signed in to the company's system, users might be logged in automatically, facilitating a seamless experience.

<Info>
Using a customer-specific Auth0 or Keycloak account requires configuration by our support team; customers cannot set it up independently.
</Info>

## User Roles 

Within MYWAI, each user can have one or more roles that determine the functionalities and areas of the platform they can access. At the time of account creation, you will be assigned a role based on the needs and functions you perform within your organization.

You can also have different roles depending on the different Companies managed by the system. It's important to be aware of your role to understand which functions are available once logged in.

## Company Selection

For larger organizations or those that manage multiple business entities, MYWAI can be configured to manage multiple Companies. If you are associated with more than one Company, upon logging in, you might be asked to select the desired Company.

After logging in with your Auth0 credentials, if the system detects that you are associated with multiple Companies, you will be presented with a list of available Companies. Select the desired one and clickc on "Continue" to access the specific working environment of that Company.

<Info>
The multi-company access page requires a special configuration that must be requested from the MYWAI support team.
</Info>
