---
title: "Certificates (v3.0)"
---

This page displays the hierarchy of **X.509 digital certificates** that implement the **PKI** (Public Key Infrastructure) securing communications among all subsystems of the MYWAI platform.

![](/images/image-6.png)

For each certificate, you can view the expiration date and thumbprint, and download the corresponding **public key** (PEM encoded .crt file) if it needs to be added to the trust list of external clients or servers.

The hierarchy starts from the Root CA (typically owned by MYWAI, so the "MYWAI Root CA"), followed by the Tenant CA (representing the specific MYWAI platform instance) and continues with any GDS and Edges installed and connected to your platform.
