# Equipments

## **Equipments Overview**

Within the MYWAI platform, Equipments are pivotal, representing tangible devices and their nuanced attributes.

## **Equipments Management Page**

* **Viewing Configured Equipments:** Upon accessing the page, users are greeted with a list of all configured equipments, providing a bird's eye view of the assets integrated into the system.
* **Equipment Details and Editing:**
  * For a deeper dive into a particular equipment's attributes, users can simply click on the 'Info' or 'Edit' button corresponding to an equipment.
  * This action triggers the appearance of a right panel, displaying all details associated with the selected equipment.
  * Furthermore, this panel facilitates the editing of equipment details.
* **Adding a New Equipment:** To enhance the system with another device, users can conveniently locate and click on the "Add new equipment" button present prominently on the page.

### **Creating and Managing Equipments**

1. **Equipment Type Selection:** Each equipment's characterization commences with a crucial step: selecting its type. This foundational step guarantees appropriate categorization and establishes inherited attributes.
2. **Detailing the Equipment:** Post type selection, users embark on detailing the equipment with its defining attributes:
   * **Name (Mandatory):** A distinct title or designation for the equipment.
   * **Description:** A succinct or elaborate explanation covering the equipment's utility, features, or importance.
   * **OPC Server URL:** For equipments interfacing with an OPC server, specify the server's URL.
   * **Site:** Denote the site, whether physical or conceptual, that hosts the equipment.
   * **Area:** Demarcate the specific zone within the denoted site accommodating the equipment.
   * **Image:** Offer a pictorial representation, like a photo or diagram, of the equipment.
   * **Location on the map:** Anchor the equipment's geographical position for enhanced logistics and planning.
3. **Incorporating** [**Sensors**](/configuration/sensors)**:** Beyond its intrinsic attributes, equipments often operate in tandem with sensors, capturing vital data, monitoring statuses, or initiating actions. Through the management page, sensors can be seamlessly linked to equipment, cementing a symbiotic relationship.
4. **Associating fields to OPC nodes**: Fields related to the Equipment Type are displayed under the respective '**Information**,' '**Monitoring**,' or '**Production**' tab. To link any of these fields to a specific OPC node, activate the '**Read from OPC**' toggle and input the Node ID.
   Please note that the tabs are not shown in case no fields are available for the specific type.
